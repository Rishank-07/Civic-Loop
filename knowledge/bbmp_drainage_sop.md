# BBMP Storm Water Drain (SWD / Raja Kaluve) SOP

## Legal Framework & Jurisdiction
- Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, Sections 288-295 (Public Drains and Drainage Works)
- National Green Tribunal (NGT) Principal Bench Directives on Raja Kaluve Buffer Zones (OA No. 22/2020)
- BBMP Chief Engineer (SWD) Annual Desilting Guidelines

## Operational Classifications
1. **Primary Drains (Raja Kaluves / Major Arterials)**:
   - Minimum buffer zone: 50 meters from center or designated cadastral boundary.
   - Maintained directly by BBMP Storm Water Drains (SWD) Department Central Division.
2. **Secondary / Tertiary Drains (Roadside Culverts & Interceptors)**:
   - Maintained by Zonal Engineering Division (Ward Assistant Executive Engineers).
   - Wastewater cross-connections fall under Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) jurisdiction.

## De-silting & Blockage Remediation Timelines
1. **Flash Flooding / Critical SWD Choke Points**:
   - Emergency response team dispatch: within 2 hours of monsoon alert.
   - De-silting machinery (Excavator / Super Sucker): mobilization within 6 hours.
2. **Routine Drainage Silt Clearance**:
   - Ward street drain de-silting cycle: Pre-monsoon (March - May) and Post-monsoon (October - November).
   - Citizen complaints regarding choked drains: SLA 48 hours for clearing physical obstructions.
3. **Citizen Verification & Disputed Closures**:
   - Official civic portal may show "Work Completed" upon physical silt excavation on road surface, but if silt remains deposited on sidewalk or falls back into drain, citizen verification must flag state as `RESOLUTION_DISPUTED`.
