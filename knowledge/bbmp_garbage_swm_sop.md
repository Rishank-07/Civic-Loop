# BBMP Solid Waste Management (SWM) Standard Operating Procedure

## Applicable Regulations
- Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike Solid Waste Management Bye-Laws (2020)
- Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 (Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change)
- Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act, 1976 & Greater Bengaluru Governance Framework

## Operational Protocols for Blackspots & Garbage Accumulation
1. **Reporting & Logging**:
   - Every complaint received via BBMP Sahaaya 2.0 or Namma Bengaluru app must be assigned a unique Grievance ID.
   - Initial dispatch to the Ward Junior Health Inspector (JHI) within 4 operational hours.
2. **Clearance SLA**:
   - Routine street garbage accumulation: 24 hours.
   - Chronic commercial blackspot dumping (> 500kg): 48 hours with secondary bobcat loader.
   - Hazardous bio/medical waste or canal perimeter dumping: immediate cordoning, clearance within 12 hours.
3. **Verification Protocol**:
   - Official closure requires photographic receipt submitted by BBMP Marshals / Auto Tipper supervisor.
   - **CivicLoop Rule**: Official BBMP status must be treated as claim until verified independently by citizen ground confirmation or timestamped geo-tagged photographic evidence.
4. **Escalation Hierarchy**:
   - Level 1: Junior Health Inspector (Ward level)
   - Level 2: Assistant Executive Engineer (Sub-division)
   - Level 3: Joint Commissioner (SWM), BBMP Central HQ, Hudson Circle
